# SJA Mobile Redesign — Changelog

Tracks changes to `SJA Mobile.dc.html`, a mobile-first, classic-UI recreation of stjoseph-apparition.org using the site's real palette (#004a9b, #004aad, #046bd2, #75c2ec, #b0d6f7) and Inknut Antiqua headings.

## History

1. **Initial build** — Home, Menu, and Congregation (inner page) screens. Real logo, slider images, copy from the site. Classic UI: solid bars, chevrons, blockquotes — no rounded cards/shadows.
2. **Fix**: hero image loaded via a static `<img>` in the template instead of `React.createElement`, so it streams/edits correctly.
3. **Fix**: hero slider image no longer cropped (was `object-fit:cover` on a fixed aspect-ratio box) — now full, uncropped image.
4. **Redesign**: replaced all 1px borders/dividers across every screen with modern drop/inset shadows (header, nav, cards, buttons, list rows, footer).
5. **Fix**: slider rebuilt as 4 real `<img>` elements in the template cross-fading via opacity (previously a single dynamic `src`, which errored on load).
6. Added `assets/angel.jpg` (apparition of the angel to St Joseph) to the Congregation page, with caption.
7. Added Emilie de Vialar portrait to the homepage Welcome section (initially with text alongside).
8. Reworked the portrait into a standalone classic oval frame (cream mat + white/blue ring), centered, no accompanying block.
9. Swapped in the user-provided painted portrait image (the file `EmiliaDeVialar.jpg` in the local folder was an infographic, not a portrait).
10. Removed the homepage hero image carousel and its static hero image entirely — homepage now goes straight from header to Welcome section.
11. **New feature**: added a "Where we are" inner-page screen with an interactive, geographically accurate world map (d3 + world-atlas topojson), highlighting the ~24 countries of SJA presence in palette colors, hover/tap tooltip showing country name, and a classic region-grouped country list below.
12. Started this changelog; will be updated after each subsequent change.
13. **Fix**: map failed to appear in the shared artifact — the external CDN scripts (d3 / topojson) and the remote map data were unavailable there. Rebuilt the map fully self-contained: the real world-map SVG from the site's own "Where we are" page (177 countries, 24 highlighted) was extracted into `assets/world-map.js` and is injected locally, with no network requests. Countries are recoloured per region from the site palette, and hover/tap tooltips read each country's name from the SVG itself.
14. **Fix (map reachability)**: at mobile width most presence countries were only 1–6px wide, so hover/tap was impossible. Added a marker layer — a visible dot plus a ~33px invisible hit circle at each of the 24 countries — and turned the "Regions of presence" list into tappable country chips (44px minimum) that highlight the country on the map with a ring and show its name.
15. **Fix**: markers and the highlight ring were all stacked at the map's top-left corner — `getBBox()` was measured while the SVG was still detached. The SVG is now inserted first and positions are computed from `getBoundingClientRect()` mapped through the SVG's screen matrix (which also handles the scaled Malta/Singapore shapes). Tapping a marker or a chip a second time now clears the highlight, and tapping empty map area clears it too.
16. **Fix (wrong country reported)**: fixed 44-unit hit circles were wider than the 2–16-unit gaps between clustered countries, so a neighbour's circle covered a marker's centre. Pins are now spread apart by a relaxation pass that keeps each pin tethered within 34 units of its own country (leader lines drawn where a pin is offset), and each hit radius is clamped to half the distance to its nearest neighbour — so every marker resolves to its own country (verified for all 24).
17. **New**: tapping a country now zooms the map to it (animated viewBox, ring drawn around the country) and opens a panel listing that country's real communities, taken from the site's own Europe/Africa, Middle East, Asia/Oceania and Latin America pages (e.g. France: Champigny, Gaillac, Marseille, Paris, Plouguenast, St Affrique, St Brieuc, Vanves). "World map" button, a second tap on the country, or tapping empty map area zooms back out. Country chips zoom too.
18. **Fix**: while a country was zoomed, hovering another country moved the ring and tooltip to it and left them latched. Map hover is now ignored while a country is selected, matching the chip behaviour — the ring stays on the country the panel is showing.
19. **New**: each community in the zoomed country panel is now a tappable row that opens a popup sheet — community name, country and region, a drag-and-drop photo slot (the dropped image persists), and the description area. The site has no per-community text or photos, so no copy was invented: the popup shows the factual line ("A community of the Sisters of St Joseph of the Apparition in Gaillac, France.") and a marked placeholder awaiting the Congregation's description.
20. **Fix (mount cost)**: the map's 157KB `assets/world-map.js` was loaded eagerly via a `<helmet>` `<script>` on every page load, and `loadMap()` ran unconditionally in `componentDidMount` — parsing/injecting the 24-country SVG even when "Where we are" was never scrolled to. The helmet script tag was removed; the map script now loads on demand (`loadScript`), gated by an `IntersectionObserver` on the map container (fires once, 800px rootMargin) so the cost is paid only when the map is about to be visible.

21. **Map replaced**: the generated SVG world map (`assets/world-map.js`, 157KB) was swapped for the Congregation's own foundations map supplied by the user (`assets/world-map-foundations.png`), which colours each country and carries the year of its first foundation. The interactive layer was rebuilt on top of it: 24 markers at coordinates measured against the artwork, hover/tap highlight, tooltip, CSS-transform zoom to the tapped country (per-country zoom level, tighter for small countries), and the same communities panel and popup as before. Marker hit areas are sized from the nearest neighbour (15–44px) so they never overlap; the region list below stays the precise selector for the crowded eastern Mediterranean. Caption notes the dates are foundation years. The lazy-loading script machinery is gone with the SVG.

22. **Foundation year on selection**: the year of the Congregation's first foundation (taken from the map artwork) now appears beside the country name in the panel header and in the map tooltip. Malta and the Palestinian Territories carry no year on the map, so none is shown for them.

23. **Fix (unclickable cluster)**: the eastern Mediterranean markers (Greece, Malta, Cyprus, Syria, Lebanon, Israel, Palestine, Jordan) and the pair of Britain/Ireland sat within a few pixels of each other at world zoom, so they could not be tapped apart. Those countries no longer get individual world-zoom markers: each group is represented by one numbered marker which zooms the map to the group and opens a chooser listing its countries with their foundation years; picking one behaves exactly like tapping a country marker. Individual markers reappear inside an open group.

24. **Marker precision (Levant)**: Greece, Malta, Cyprus, Lebanon, Israel, Palestine and Jordan were re-measured on a 5.6x magnification of the artwork — each had sat slightly west, in the sea off the coast — and now fall on their own territory.

25. **Fix (unreachable France, hit sizing)**: hit areas were sized by a loop over all country points, including pins that were hidden inside a group, so France shrank to 15px because of the hidden United Kingdom pin — while the 46px "Britain & Ireland" badge sitting 17px away (and above it in z-order) swallowed the tap. Marker geometry is now built once, over the markers actually on screen (visible pins plus cluster badges), with a floor of 18px; visible pins sit above cluster badges; the Britain & Ireland badge moved out over the Atlantic (34.4/24.2); and Myanmar, Thailand and Singapore — 13px apart at world zoom — became a third group, "South-East Asia".
26. **New (pan and double-tap)**: when the map is zoomed into a country or a group it can be dragged to move around (pointer drag, clamped to the image edges, transition suppressed while dragging, and a drag never registers as a marker tap), and a double-tap anywhere on the map returns to the world view. `touch-action` is only disabled while zoomed, so ordinary page scrolling over the world map is unaffected.

27. **Group (Central America)**: Guatemala and Panama sit 18px apart at world zoom, below the touch minimum, so they became a fourth group with its badge in the Pacific.

28. **Interactive set tied to the communities data**: markers, group badges, group chooser rows and region chips are now derived from `LOCATIONS` — a country is linked only while it has at least one community listed. Countries on the artwork that belong to the Congregation's history but have no community today stay coloured and dated, with no link. Removing a country's communities from the data removes its link everywhere, with no further edits.

29. **Fix (Malta looked broken)**: Malta and the Palestinian Territories are not drawn or labelled on the artwork, so selecting them zoomed onto empty sea. Their markers now carry a small name label, and their zoom is pulled back (Malta 3.6x) so Sicily and the Tunisian coast stay in frame for context. Selection itself was working — the four Maltese communities were listed all along.

30. **Fix (marker taps swallowed while zoomed)**: the pan handler called `setPointerCapture` on pointerdown, which retargets the following `click` to the map container — so tapping a country marker inside an open group (Malta, and every other member) did nothing. Capture is now taken only once the pointer has actually moved past the 6px drag threshold, and released on pointerup; panning is unchanged and taps reach their markers again.

31. **Fix (Israel's marker selected Palestine)**: the two points are 0.3% apart on the artwork, so at every zoom their 18px hit boxes overlapped and the later sibling won the tap. The hit-size floor dropped to 12px so a box can never cover a neighbour's centre, and the Palestinian Territories marker is now offset 34x20px with a leader line back to its true position — its label reads "Palestine" and, like Malta's, sits above the dot instead of over the artwork's own text.

32. **The rest of the pages (site build, `docs/`)**: all 36 remaining pages of the navigation were ported from the WordPress mirror by a new generator, `tools/build_pages.py` — section landing pages, the Foundress and Charism texts, the five regional community lists, the ministries, formation, news, bulletins, the General Chapter 2025 record, the weblinks, contact and the members area. Carousels became swipeable galleries, images were re-encoded to WebP (165 files, 15MB), the PDFs the mirror holds are served locally, videos play from the live site. The drawer now carries an href for every entry (plus Safeguarding, which the live menu has and the canvas did not), the footer links to the section pages, and the home page's pending links point at real pages. Details in `docs/README.md`.

33. **Reader, search, lazy parts, map (site build)**: PDFs served by the site open in an in-page reader (pdf.js) instead of downloading; the search box is on every page and suggests pages and passages from a content index built with the pages; pages over 11KB are split into parts fetched as the reader scrolls (Current news, 9 parts); the Contact page carries a Google Maps embed of the Generalate with an "Open in Google Maps" link.

34. **Image viewer (site build)**: tapping a photograph opens it full-screen with caption and count; previous/next by button, arrow key or swipe through every photograph on the page.

35. **All PDFs served locally (site build)**: the 18 PDFs the mirror lacked (bulletins 1–10 and the 2024 news reflections) were fetched from the live site, so every PDF button on the site now reads "Read (PDF)" and opens in the reader.

36. **Document cards (site build)**: cover + PDF button pairs became cards — the cover opens the reader, "Read online" and "Download PDF" sit beneath it; image-file and dead links handled; stale lazily-loaded parts are cleared on each build; CSS/JS links carry a content hash.

37. **PDF first page as cover (site build)**: a document card without a cover in the source (Safeguarding) now shows the PDF's own first page, rendered at build time.

38. **Document cards (site build)**: the "Read online" button was removed where the cover already opens the reader; only "Download PDF" remains beneath the cover.

39. **Bulletin no. 13 (site build)**: the mirror's Bulletins page was refreshed from the live site, which had gained Bulletin no. 13 – September 2026 after the mirror was taken; its PDF and cover were fetched and the page rebuilt (12 issues; no. 5 was never published).

40. **Latest news on Home (site build)**: the three items are now generated from the newest dated entries of Bulletins and Current news on every build; currently Bulletin no. 13 (Sept 2026), Vocations Awareness Week (Aug 2026), Saint Emilie's room (Jul 2026).

41. **Contact call-out on Home (site build)**: the Contact row in Explore became a deep-blue panel between Explore and Latest news — "Get in touch", phone, e-mail and address with icons, a white "Write to us" button and a "Find us on the map" link to the map on the Contact page (two-column on desktop).

42. **Contact panel folded into the footer (site build)**: the Home call-out became the top half of the shared footer on every page — contact details, "Write to us", then name, section links and copyright on a darker band.

43. **Footer as one block (site build)**: the darker lower band is gone — one gradient, thin rules between the contact part, the name and links, and the copyright; "We are happy to hear from you" and the Congregation's name share one size (20px, 22px on desktop) and every other line is 15px.

44. **Footer menu (site build)**: the Congregation's name was dropped from the footer; the section links are pill-shaped buttons with a hover state and sit on one row with the (shortened) copyright line on desktop.

45. **New accurate blue map (design + site build)**: the foundations artwork was replaced by a map generated from Natural Earth country boundaries (`assets/world-map-blue.svg`, Mercator, self-contained, no network). The 24 countries of presence are shaded in the region blues; countries of earlier foundations (Canada, USA, Haiti, Algeria, Libya, Egypt, Sudan, Mauritania, Turkey, Armenia, Bulgaria, former Yugoslavia, Argentina) in pale blue; all others white. Every marker and group badge is now placed by projecting the country's real longitude/latitude, so each sits on its own territory; the map note explains the two blues. `js/map.js` now counts toward the CSS/JS content hash, so a change to it busts browser caches like the other files. The old `world-map-foundations.png` was removed.

46. **Fix (Palestine stranded in the desert, square focus box)**: the 34x20px nudge carried over from the old artwork put the Palestine marker over Jordan/Saudi Arabia on the real map, and because crowding was measured at the nudged position it stood alone outside the Holy Land group. Crowding now uses true positions, so Palestine joins the Mediterranean & Holy Land badge at every width; when the group is open it is pulled west into the sea (−30, −8px) with its leader line back to the West Bank, clear of Israel, Jordan and Lebanon. Markers and badges no longer show the browser's square focus outline around their hit box after a tap; keyboard focus rings the dot or badge instead.

47. **Search on the map (site build)**: a round search button at the map's top-right opens a field for finding a country or community by name, accent-blind and with common names (Palestine, UK, Britain, England, Burma). Matches drop below the field in the site-search suggestion style; choosing a country zooms to it, choosing a community also opens its sheet. Keyboard: arrows, Enter, Escape. The map card no longer clips, so the list can overlap the note on a phone-sized map; the field keeps to 420px on wide screens.

48. **One blue for presence (design + site build)**: the 24 countries of presence on `assets/world-map-blue.svg` were shaded in six region blues (`#004a9b` … `#75c2ec`), which contradicted the map note's "Deep blue: present today" and made Australia and Latin America look close to the pale blue of earlier foundations. They are now all deep blue `#004a9b`; pale `#b0d6f7` for earlier foundations is unchanged. The region colours stay on the region list's dots.

49. **No region colours (site build)**: the region list under the map no longer carries the six region blues — every region dot and every highlighted country chip is the site's deep blue `#004a9b` (a highlighted Australia chip was white text on light `#75c2ec`). The colour constants were removed from `js/map.js`.

50. **Design brought in line with the site (design)**: `SJA Mobile.dc.html` now carries the changes made on the site in 46–49 — the Palestine marker is pulled west into the sea (−30, −8px) when its group is open; markers, badges and the search button show a focus ring on the dot instead of the browser's square outline; the map has the same search button and field (accent-blind, common names, arrows/Enter/Escape, a community opens its sheet); and the region colour constants are gone, with region dots and highlighted chips in deep blue `#004a9b`. The design already grouped Palestine at world view, so the width-adaptive crowding fix (46) has no counterpart here.

51. **Earlier foundations named on the map (site build)**: hovering or tapping one of the 13 pale-blue countries shows its name and year of first foundation, with "Earlier foundation · no community today" beneath, and tints the country. Years read from the Congregation's foundations map: Algeria 1835, Armenia 1852, Libya 1854, Egypt 1866, Sudan 1873, Bulgaria 1886, Turkey 1887, United States 1930, Mauritania 1959, Haiti 1968, Argentina 1969, Canada 2003; former Yugoslavia carries no year there. The countries are also in the map search (with Serbia, Croatia… USA, Türkiye as alternative names). Hit shapes are generated by `tools/earlier_foundations.py` into `js/earlier-foundations.js` (21KB); the generator's cache-buster now covers that file and hyphenated script names. Map labels now stay inside the frame, sliding in from the edges and dropping below their point near the top. The map note says the pale countries can be tapped.

52. **Earlier foundations in the design (design)**: `SJA Mobile.dc.html` now names the 13 pale-blue countries on hover or tap, with the same years, tint, second line, search entries and alternative names as the site (51), and the same map note. Its labels stay inside the map frame too. The hit shapes come from `assets/earlier-foundations.js`, which `tools/earlier_foundations.py` now writes alongside the site's copy.

53. **UK and Ireland apart on wide maps (design + site build)**: their markers sat 22px apart on the full-width map, under the 26px needed for a marker of its own, so they always shared the Britain & Ireland badge. The UK marker moved to Grantham (0.6°W 52.9°N) and Ireland's to Tuam (9.0°W 53.5°N), both still inland: 26px apart at 968px and 28px at 1044px, so tablets in landscape and desktops show them individually. Narrower maps keep the badge (8px apart on a phone). The design file takes the same coordinates; its phone-width map groups them as before.

54. **Palestine and Malta on their own territory (design + site build)**: the Palestine marker is no longer offset into the sea; it sits on the West Bank, and Israel's moved to the Negev (34.8°E 30.6°N) to give them room. Inside the Mediterranean group, the countries still too close at its zoom get a nested "Holy Land" badge (Israel, Palestine, Jordan, Lebanon on a phone; Israel and Palestine on a desktop) that zooms in until they are 26px apart — about 14x on a phone. Selecting Malta or the Palestinian Territories zooms in until the territory is about 40px wide (up to 60x); on a phone Malta's selected marker becomes a ring round the island. Malta and Gozo, smaller than their own border line, are outlined in deep blue on `world-map-blue.svg` so they no longer read as pale. The leader-line code is gone from both the site and the design file (and with it the design file's `<line>` console errors).

55. **Group badges say what they count (design + site build)**: hovering a group badge, or reaching it with the keyboard, shows a label above it — "7 countries" (or "1 country"), with the group's name on a quieter second line ("Mediterranean & Holy Land", "Holy Land"…). The badges themselves still show only the number; the label goes as soon as the pointer or focus leaves, and opening the group clears it.

56. **No "Palestine" tag on the map (design + site build)**: the permanent name tag above the Palestinian Territories marker is gone; it dates from the old artwork, which neither drew nor named the territory. The marker now sits on the West Bank like any other, and hover or selection still names it. Malta keeps its tag.

57. **No "Malta" tag either (design + site build)**: Malta's permanent name tag is gone too, so no marker carries one; the tag code (`UNLABELLED`, `.pin__label`) was removed from the site and the design file. Hover and selection still name every country.

58. **Every marker in the Holy Land view (design + site build)**: zoomed into the Holy Land group, only its own countries had markers, so Cyprus, Syria, Lebanon, Jordan, Greece, Malta, Italy and Tunisia sat bare in frame. Every country in frame now keeps its marker, unless it is still crowded at that zoom; the group's own members always show, so they win any tie.

59. **Malta's marker reads as on Malta (design + site build)**: in the Mediterranean view Malta is about 6px across, so the marker's 11px dot hid it completely and the marker seemed to float in the sea south of Sicily (it was placed exactly on the island). A marker over a territory narrower than its dot is now drawn hollow — a white ring with a clear centre — so the island shows inside it, matching how the other markers read over their deep-blue countries.

60. **Zoom buttons and map key (design + site build)**: + and − buttons at the map's bottom-left zoom by 2x around the frame's centre (1x to 60x), and a pinch, trackpad pinch or Ctrl + scroll zooms freely around the fingers or pointer; a plain scroll still scrolls the page. Zoomed in on the world by hand, groups split as their countries spread apart. A selected territory smaller than its dot (Malta, zoomed right out) is drawn hollow instead of as the large ring. A key under the map shows deep blue for communities today, pale blue for earlier foundations, a country marker and a group badge; the note below it drops the colour sentences it now repeats.

61. **Live count in the map key (design + site build)**: the key's group badge no longer shows a fixed 3; it carries the count of the largest group badge on the map at that moment (7 on a phone's world view, falling as you zoom in), and the "Group of countries" entry leaves the key while no group badge is shown (a country selected, or zoomed in far enough that every group has split).

62. **Group badges in their own blue (design + site build)**: group badges, on the map and in the key, are now filled with the brighter palette blue #046bd2 instead of the country markers' #004a9b, so a group never reads as a single country. White on #046bd2 keeps a 5:1 contrast for the count.

63. **Country markers match the group badges (design + site build)**: country markers (the dot, the hollow ring and the key's dot) now use the same brighter blue #046bd2 as the group badges; a hovered or selected marker still turns deep #00305f.

64. **Zooming out regroups (design + site build)**: zooming out with − or a pinch now brings the group badges back. Zoomed out past an open group's own zoom, the map leaves the group (its chooser closes) and its countries regroup under their badge; zoomed out past a selected country's own zoom, crowding is measured at the zoom shown, so the other countries regroup while the selected one keeps its own marker (and is left out of its group's count). A crowded country stays visible whenever its group has no badge to stand in for it.
